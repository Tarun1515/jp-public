Privacy policy

What personal data Staffroom India collects, why, and what is done with it.

Draft — requires legal review before launch

The section headings below are the structure this document needs, including the obligations that India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 places on a platform of this kind. The wording under each heading is a plain-language placeholder written to make the page reviewable. It has not been drafted or checked by a lawyer, and this page is not a compliant privacy notice as it stands.

1. Who is responsible for your data

Will identify Staffroom India as the entity determining how personal data is processed, with its registered details and a contact point for privacy questions.

2. What we collect

Will list each category and be specific rather than gesturing at "certain information":

  • Account details — name, email address, mobile number, password (stored only as a hash).
  • Teacher profile — subjects, levels, qualifications, experience, preferred locations.
  • School details — institution, branches, and the person authorised to hire.
  • Application activity — which vacancies you applied to and the stage each reached.
  • Technical data — IP address, device and browser, and log entries needed to run the service securely.

3. Why we collect it, and on what basis

Will state the purpose for each category and the lawful basis for processing it, distinguishing what is necessary to provide the service from anything that requires separate consent.

4. Who can see your data

Will explain what a school sees when a teacher applies, what it does not see, and that a teacher's details are not visible to schools they have not applied to. Will list any processors — hosting, email delivery — and confirm that personal data is not sold.

5. How long we keep it

Will give retention periods per category, including what happens to applications after a vacancy closes and to an account after it is deleted.

6. Your rights

Will set out the rights of a Data Principal under the DPDP Act — access, correction, erasure, grievance redressal, and nominating another person to exercise them — with the process and timeframe for each.

7. Security

Will describe the safeguards in place, including that passwords are stored only as salted hashes and never in a form that can be reversed, and how a personal data breach would be reported.

8. Cookies and similar technologies

Will list what is stored in the browser and why. At present the platform stores the session token needed to keep you signed in; it does not run advertising trackers.

9. Children

Will state the minimum age for holding an account and how that is handled, given the additional obligations the DPDP Act places on processing a child's data.

10. Changes to this policy

Will state how changes are notified and when they take effect.

11. Contact and grievances

Will name the grievance officer and how to reach them. General questions can go through our contact page. See also the terms of use.
