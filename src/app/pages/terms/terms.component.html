Terms of use

The terms that govern using Staffroom India as a teacher or as a school.

Draft — requires legal review before launch

The section headings below are the structure this document needs. The wording under each is a plain-language placeholder written to make the page reviewable. It has not been drafted or checked by a lawyer and is not enforceable as it stands. Staffroom India must supply or approve the final wording before this page is published.

1. Who these terms apply to

Will define the parties: Staffroom India as the operator of the platform, teachers who create a profile, and schools that register to hire. Will state that creating an account means accepting these terms.

2. Accounts and eligibility

Will cover the minimum age and eligibility to work, that account details must be accurate, that one person holds one teacher account, and that a school account is held on behalf of a named institution by someone authorised to act for it.

3. School verification

Will state what verification checks and — importantly — what it does not. Verification confirms that a school exists and that the registering person is connected to it. It is not an inspection, an endorsement, or a warranty about a school as an employer.

4. What you may post

Will cover accuracy of vacancies and profiles, prohibited content, and discrimination in job adverts — including which characteristics may not be used to filter applicants under Indian law.

5. Applications, offers and the hiring relationship

Will make clear that any employment contract is between the school and the teacher, and that Staffroom India is not a party to it, not an employment agency for these purposes, and not responsible for salary, terms or conduct after an offer.

6. Fees

Will state what is free and what is charged, who is charged, and the billing and refund terms. Pricing is not finalised — this section cannot be completed until it is.

7. Acceptable use

Will cover scraping, bulk extraction of contact details, misuse of applicant data, automated access, and attempts to interfere with the service.

8. Suspension and termination

Will set out when an account may be suspended or removed, what notice is given, and what happens to live vacancies and in-flight applications.

9. Intellectual property

Will cover ownership of the platform itself, and the licence users grant for the content they submit so it can be displayed to the other side.

10. Liability

Will set out the limits of liability, including for hiring decisions, the accuracy of profiles and vacancies, and interruptions to the service. Must be drafted by a lawyer.

11. Changes to these terms

Will state how changes are notified and when they take effect.

12. Governing law and disputes

Will state the governing law and the jurisdiction for disputes.

13. Contact

Questions about these terms go to our contact page. See also the privacy policy, which covers personal data.
