Every school is checked before it can post

Anyone can put a teaching job on the internet. Not everyone who does is a school.

Before a school's first vacancy appears here, we confirm the school exists, that the person posting works there, and that it is what it claims to be. A teacher applying through Staffroom is applying to a school that has been through that check.

It makes the first day slower for a school. It is also the reason a teacher can take what they read here seriously.
