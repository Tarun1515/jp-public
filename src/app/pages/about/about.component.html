About Staffroom India

A hiring platform for schools and teachers, built around one rule: a school is checked before its vacancies are published.

The problem we started from

Teaching jobs in India are advertised in a lot of places. Very few of those places check who is doing the advertising. A teacher answering an advert often cannot tell whether the school is real, whether the role exists, or whether the person replying works there.

The cost of that falls almost entirely on the teacher. They send the same details out repeatedly, follow up by phone, and hear nothing back — with no way to tell an application that was rejected from one that was never read.

What we do differently

Every school on Staffroom India is verified before its first vacancy goes live. We confirm the school exists, and that the person posting is connected to it. It is a one-time check, not a per-job one, and it happens before anything that school posts is visible to a teacher.

That makes the first day slower for a school, and we think that is the right trade. It is the difference between a listing a teacher has to assess and one they can act on.

What a teacher gets

  • One profile, written once, sent with every application.
  • An application that takes one step and does not need chasing.
  • A visible stage for every application, including when the answer is no.

What a school gets

  • Applicants who saw the subject, level and terms before applying.
  • One list to shortlist from, instead of an inbox.
  • A record of who decided what, so a vacancy closes with an outcome attached.

Where this is going

The platform is being built in stages. Accounts, verification and hiring come first; public job search follows. We would rather ship a part that works than a page that looks complete and is not connected to anything.
